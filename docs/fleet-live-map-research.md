# Fleet live-map research

Research date: 2026-09-20. Scope: an owner/manager live-map **interface shell** before a GPS provider, tracker model, device-to-vehicle link, location feed, or route-history API is known. This is product and implementation research, not proof that tracking works or legal approval for 24/7 monitoring.

## Evidence boundary in this repository

- The current [`Vehicle`](../src/types/vehicle.ts) contract contains fleet identity, branch, category, booking availability, documents, and maintenance, but no position, last-fix time, or tracker identity. [`Branch`](../src/types/branch.ts) has a free-text `location`, not coordinates. The frontend now includes Leaflet for a basemap, but **no vehicle marker, route, branch centre, signal timestamp, or “live” count can be derived from existing data**.
- A vehicle's `active` or `available_for_booking` state is an operational fleet state, **not** evidence of motion, an online tracker, or a current location; this follows from the distinct fields in [`Vehicle`](../src/types/vehicle.ts). Do not infer GPS state from driving-session schedules either.
- The approved product scope limits future position/route access to owner and manager in their allowed company/branch. The UI route guard is not sufficient: every eventual vehicle/location/history request needs server-side object-level authorization, as [OWASP API1:2023](https://api-security.owasp.org/editions/2023/en/0xa1-broken-object-level-authorization/) explicitly warns for APIs accepting object IDs. This is a recommendation for the future API, not a claim that such an API exists today.

## Map renderer, tiles, and attribution

- [MapLibre GL JS](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/) is a browser/WebGL renderer controlled by a style and tile source; its [source license](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) is BSD-style. **A renderer is not a basemap service or a GPS provider.** Its demo styles/tiles are documented as demo/test assets in the [MapLibre demotiles repository](https://github.com/maplibre/demotiles), so they are not evidence of a supported production tile service.
- [OpenStreetMap data](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) is ODbL-licensed and needs attribution. OSM Foundation's [raster-tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) and [vector-tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/vector/) distinguish free data from capacity-limited public tile servers: visible attribution, normal browser identification/referrer, cache compliance, and no bulk/pre-fetch are required; availability is best-effort with no SLA and access may be blocked. **Recommendation:** keep the basemap source configurable and choose a licensed hosted or self-hosted tile source before enabling a persistent 24/7 production map. Do not hard-wire a public OSM tile endpoint as an assumed production entitlement.
- If MapLibre is selected later, retain visible tile/data attribution in the map layout. [MapLibre's attribution control](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/classes/AttributionControl/) is available, but OSM's policy says attribution must not be hidden under overlays or off-screen. A no-tiles/no-position shell needs no fake map imagery or attribution.

## Recommended UX for the provider-free shell

1. Use the existing AutoDrive typography, colors, and components, not a copied Geotab/Wialon skin. The primary task is **find a vehicle and understand tracking readiness**: branch filter (owner: permitted branches; manager: scoped branch), search by plate/model, vehicle list, and a bounded detail/empty-state area. This arrangement is inferred from the verified [Geotab map](https://support.geotab.com/help/mygeotab/fleet-activity/map/the-map), not validated AutoDrive user research.
2. On today's data, show an explicit **“GPS ulanmagan / tracking not configured”** state. The list may contain real fleet records, but no map marker, arbitrary city-centred camera, “online”, “moving”, “0 on map”, last-signal timestamp, or route line. Distinguish `no vehicles in scope`, `vehicle exists but tracking is unconfigured`, `loading`, `authorization denied`, and `request failed`; none should masquerade as a live feed. This is a recommendation forced by the [current vehicle/branch contracts](../src/types/vehicle.ts).
3. Reserve a second **route-history** mode, but do not show a functioning playback bar or a fabricated sample route. Once a future provider API is known, select vehicle and bounded date range first, then show a chronological route/trip list and selected geometry. The interaction follows the first-party [Geotab history](https://support.geotab.com/help/mygeotab/fleet-activity/trips/trips-history) and [Wialon history](https://help.wialon.com/en/wialon-mobile/ios/user-guide/monitoring/unit-history) references. The previously agreed 90-day detail retention is an AutoDrive product rule, **not** a legal period inferred from those sources.
4. For a future feed, display the source-reported observation time and the server receipt time if both are available; show “last known” or “signal unavailable” rather than “live” when uncertain. Define freshness thresholds only after the provider's expected reporting cadence and latency are measured. This is a design recommendation informed by Geotab's distinct [not-communicating state](https://support.geotab.com/help/mygeotab/fleet-activity/map/the-map), not a claim about AutoDrive devices.
5. Do not add browser geolocation: that would locate the **viewer**, not the school vehicle. [MapLibre GeolocateControl](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/classes/GeolocateControl/) exists for a user's own device, which is a different job.

## Accessibility and responsive acceptance

- A vehicle list/details view must carry the same identity, signal status, and time as any future markers; the map cannot be the sole way to access information. This is the practical application of [WCAG 2.2 non-text alternatives](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-content) and [keyboard operation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/keyboard). MapLibre offers [keyboard pan/zoom](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/classes/KeyboardHandler/), but it does not replace keyboard-accessible list selection and labeled controls.
- Search/loading/no-result/error changes should be perceivable without stealing focus; [WCAG 2.2 status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages) covers programmatic notification. Signal changes every few seconds should **not** be announced repeatedly by an aggressive live region; announce user-initiated filter results and meaningful errors (design recommendation).
- Give filter, list, and history controls usable touch targets. [WCAG 2.2 target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum) sets a 24×24 CSS-pixel minimum or spacing exceptions and discusses dense map pins; use the separate list as an equivalent accessible action. On narrow screens, place the list and details in a deliberate switch/stack without obscuring attribution or controls (layout recommendation based on the same requirements and [OSM attribution policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)).

**Shortlist, not a winner by assertion.** For a quick licensed pilot, evaluate **MapLibre or Leaflet with a contracted tile provider** (the renderer comparison follows below); for local address/POI quality, evaluate **2GIS** side by side; keep **Google Maps JS** as a globally documented benchmark. The eventual GPS trace must be overlaid from the tracker and compared independently; neither a nice basemap nor a routing API proves a student's drive.

Do not send names, plates or route coordinates to geocoder/search URLs by default. Neither this comparison nor a hosted provider's availability is legal approval for 24/7 tracking.

## Leaflet renderer assessment (research update: 2026-09-20)

**Decision (2026-09-20):** The product owner selected Leaflet as AutoDrive's map renderer. This does not select a basemap/tile product, approve its commercial terms, or supply GPS data. Keep those decisions separate before enabling a production map.

**Basemap follow-up (2026-09-20):** The product owner approved OpenStreetMap data. The local-development map uses `tile.openstreetmap.org` with visible attribution for ordinary interactive viewing; it adds no bulk download or offline prefetch. Production has no public-OSM fallback; `VITE_FLEET_MAP_TILE_URL` must point to a licensed or self-hosted OSM-derived raster service, with additional provider attribution configured where required. If unset, the production UI shows an explicit map-source-unconfigured state. This is not GPS integration and does not add vehicle markers or routes. [OSMF tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/).

**Documented capabilities.** [Leaflet](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet) is a BSD-2-Clause browser mapping library, not a basemap or GPS data source; its [core feature list](https://leafletjs.com/) includes raster tile/WMS layers, markers, popups, polylines and GeoJSON, along with touch gestures and keyboard navigation. The [API reference](https://leafletjs.com/reference.html) documents updating a marker with `setLatLng` and replacing route-line coordinates with `setLatLngs`, enough to render a future authorized vehicle-position feed and selected historical trace. That says nothing about whether a tracker exists or whether streets in Uzbekistan are accurate. The official [download page](https://leafletjs.com/download.html) lists **1.9.4 (May 2023) as stable** and 2.0.0-alpha.1 (August 2025) as a prerelease; use the stable API for a pilot unless 2.x and plugin compatibility are specifically validated. The library license is separate from every tile provider's data, fees and attribution obligations ([Leaflet license](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/blob/main/LICENSE), [OSM tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)).

**Important provider-specific corrections to the earlier shortlist:**

- **2GIS:** The [cloud _vector_ Map Tiles API](https://docs.2gis.com/en/maps/others/maptiles/overview) requires MapGL. Separately, [2GIS _Raster_ Tiles API](https://docs.2gis.com/en/maps/others/rastertiles/overview) exposes a PNG endpoint for third-party libraries. Its [overview explicitly warns](https://docs.2gis.com/en/maps/overview) that third-party raster compatibility is **not guaranteed and may break without notice**. A Leaflet + 2GIS raster spike is therefore possible but not equivalent to a supported MapGL integration. The raster service bills successful 256×256 **tile requests**, not Leaflet map loads ([billing](https://docs.2gis.com/en/platform-manager/subscription/pricing)).
- **Yandex:** Its [Tiles API product page](https://yandex.com/maps-api/products/tiles-api) explicitly names Leaflet/OpenLayers. Published Basic pricing starts at **USD 450/year for fewer than 250,000 tile requests** (and can incur overage); this is a _different product and meter_ from the earlier USD 10,000/year JavaScript API tier. [Yandex's tile request reference](https://yandex.com/maps-api/docs/tiles-api/request.html) defaults to ellipsoidal `wgs84_mercator`, while [Leaflet defaults to spherical `EPSG:3857`](https://leafletjs.com/reference.html); request `projection=web_mercator` for Leaflet's default CRS so tile roads and GPS overlays line up. The tile API's supported caption-language list does **not** include `uz_UZ`; Leaflet cannot create missing Uzbek labels. Its [logo-placement rule](https://yandex.com/maps-api/docs/tiles-api/index.html) and the commercial monitoring license still apply.
- **Google:** Leaflet can in principle consume 2D imagery from the separate [Map Tiles API](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile), and Google's [policy discusses third-party renderers](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies); no first-party Leaflet integration example was established here. This is **not** the Maps JavaScript API price: [2D Map Tiles](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing) has a 100,000-tile monthly no-charge cap and then USD 0.60/1,000 in its first paid band, while [usage](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/usage-and-billing) is per tile request. Integration must obtain/refresh a [session token](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/session_tokens) and render the [Google logo plus viewport-dependent copyright](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies), so it is not merely pasting a tile URL into `L.tileLayer`. Confirm terms and prototype before calling it an approved Leaflet pairing.

**Engineering inference, not a final provider decision:** Put Leaflet on the two-branch pilot shortlist if AutoDrive chiefly needs a 2D map, selectable vehicle markers, and an observed GPS polyline. Prefer a MapLibre spike when vector style/language control and built-in GeoJSON clustering are material; prefer provider-native SDKs when their terms or support conditions require them. With no pilot coordinates, tracker payload, usage volume, or license contracts, no renderer/provider combination can honestly be ranked first for Uzbekistan. Reuse the existing no-GPS shell until those release gates are met.

## Privacy and release gates

- A route can reveal a vehicle's and potentially a staff member's movement. The [Uzbekistan personal-data law](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-4396419) governs stated processing purpose, access tied to duties, subject information, and retention; this note does **not** determine a lawful basis or declare monitoring lawful. Before enabling 24/7 tracking, obtain the planned legal review and staff notice. Apply the approved company/branch scope on every future location/history API and test cross-tenant/branch denial, per [OWASP's object-level authorization guidance](https://api-security.owasp.org/editions/2023/en/0xa1-broken-object-level-authorization/).
- Do not send person names, plate numbers, or route payloads to a public tile URL, and do not place live coordinates in analytics, browser URL/hash, or a public share link by default. These are privacy-by-design recommendations supported by the [OSM Foundation tile-service privacy warning](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) and the [personal-data law](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-4396419); exact vendor data-processing terms remain undecided.
- **Not releasable as live tracking yet:** provider/device contract, authenticated sample payloads, coordinate/clock semantics, device-to-vehicle matching, report cadence, historical API, tile-service terms, authorization/RLS tests, 90-day deletion proof, legal review, and real two-branch pilot are still unknown or unverified. The shell may be implemented and tested now, clearly labeled as unconfigured.
